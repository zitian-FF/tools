In this update, a brand-new crystal event is about to begin.

Commanders can obtain amethysts Crystal through the Crystal Factory and Crystal Boss, and exchange them for related rewards in the Crystal Shop.

Silver Bricks can also be obtained in the Crystal Shop and used to redeem newly added bundles in the Bullseye Loot, Glittering Market, and Bounty Hunter events.
