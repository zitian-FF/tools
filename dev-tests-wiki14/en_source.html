#105 1v1 | Love Across the Other Side of the Earth
“💌 Contributor Introduction



This story comes from Duckƴ, a player from Server 105 and the 1v1 alliance in Last War.

For Duckƴ, Last War is more than just a game that accompanies daily life.
It is also the place where a special bond began. "
“🌍 Their Story

Below is the story Duckƴ shared with us.
We first met in January 2024, on the same Server in Last War.

At first, we actually got to know each other as enemies.
But because we were both R4, we began communicating frequently and closely, and gradually became closer because of it.

In April 2024, he was the first to approach me and said he wanted me to teach him Korean.
Later, our conversations extended from the game to Discord.
We started having voice calls and video calls, and also began sharing our daily lives with each other.

As if drawn by some kind of force, I bought a plane ticket to Brazil just to go see him.
Because he was the one who first gathered the courage, I also wanted to show him my courage.

So, for the first time in my life, I traveled to Brazil.
I spent two dreamlike weeks of vacation with him, and after that, we became a couple.

Even though we were physically far apart, we could meet in Last War every day, so that distance did not feel so long.
I did not know English, and he did not know Korean, but as long as we used Last War’s Translate feature, communication was not a problem at all.

Later, in October 2024, he ended the business he had been running in Brazil, sold his car, and came to Korea for me.

Since October 2024, we have continued this bond.
Whether on days when we argued or days when we stayed by each other’s side, we always played Last War together.

And it is also because of Last War that sometimes our arguments would quickly be resolved; through the process of helping each other, our love also grew deeper.
Because of the bond that Last War brought us together through, we registered our marriage in June 2026 and became legally married.

I once believed that there would be no true love in my life, nor any destined bond.
But it turned out that my love was on the other side of the Earth.
And what connected this bond was Last War.

To Last War, the game that became the game of my life and also allowed me to meet my life partner, I offer my endless gratitude!”
“💚 A Message from Last War

from enemies at first, to lovers later, and now to becoming spouses hand in hand.
